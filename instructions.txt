What the game is like
* the game is a combination top down and platformer
* the player starts out top down like Pokémon and walks around. They walk up a wooded path with grass on either side of a dirt road. There are people standing along the side of the road and when one sees you they approach you and says something along the lines of "you you can't go any further until you play my cyber jump!"
* the game then enters a 2D platformer similar to Mario. The player has to jump across a series of platformer and defeating enemies by jumping on their heads. If they reach the goal at the end of the level (represented by a checkered flag) they win it. If they die either by touching the side of an enemy or falling out the bottom, they lose a life and restart the platform. They have three lives. Once they're out of all of their lives, they lose it.
* when the player loses a 2-D platform, it cuts back to the top down world and the person gets thrown all the way back to the start. If they win the 2-D platform, the person who talked to them congratulates them and allows them to continue walking down the path. Overall the path has five people each with their own unique level.
